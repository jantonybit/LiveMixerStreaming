Privacy Policy

LiveMixer Streaming · Sound Designer by Jantonybit

Effective 22 November 2026 · Version 1.0 · Publisher: Jantonybit

LiveMixer Streaming runs entirely on your computer. It does not create accounts, collect personal data, send telemetry or show ads, and it never records or uploads your audio. Audio is processed in memory and sent only to the outputs you select in the app.

1. Who is responsible

The app is published by Jantonybit on the Microsoft Store under the identity Jantonybit.LiveMixerStreaming. For questions, open an issue in the project's GitHub repository (link on the Store page).

2. Data we collect

None. There is no sign-in, telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, usage counters, cookies, advertising IDs or third-party SDKs. The app does not access contacts, personal files, location, camera or photos, and it never transfers audio or other content to servers.

The app is free, has no in-app purchases and does not ask for donations.

3. Audio and devices (microphone permission)

AccessPurposeWhere the audio goes
ASIO / WASAPI inputsReceive mixer channel signals (microphones and audio interface)Processed in memory; goes to the ASIO outputs and/or Master Out you choose
WASAPI playback loopbackCapture what Windows sends to a playback device ("PC Audio")Same local processing; never written to disk
Per-app captureCapture the audio of a specific running programSame local processing; streaming apps and LiveMixer itself are ignored
MIDI devicesControl mixer parameters with physical controllersLocal commands only; nothing is sent over the network

Audio is not archived, encoded for upload or used to train models. The options "Don't hear on PC" and "Divert Windows apps" only temporarily mute Windows devices/sessions while the engine runs and restore the original state when you stop or close the app.

Low-latency mode requires an ASIO driver installed by your interface manufacturer. LiveMixer Streaming does not install drivers or redistribute third-party drivers.

4. Data stored on your computer

The app only saves usage settings as text files next to the executable (or in the package's virtualized local storage when installed as MSIX): config\ui.cfg, config\mixer.cfg, config\midi.map, config\promos.cfg and config\scenes\*. These files are never uploaded. Uninstall the app or delete the config folder to remove them.

5. Internet and links

The app makes no network requests on its own. The only internet-related action is opening Microsoft Store pages (links to other apps by the same publisher) in the Store app or your default browser, and only after an explicit click. The links live in config\promos.cfg and can be deleted by you. There are no automatic pop-ups and no links opened without interaction.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not share data with third parties. There are no ad networks, analytics services, cloud services or payment processors in the app. Microsoft may handle information about your Microsoft account and installation according to the Microsoft Store policies, since the app is distributed there.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect children's information.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date at the top.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the LiveMixer Streaming GitHub repository (link on the Store page).
